    Method

    Data. 707,107 regular-season pitches from 829 MLB pitchers (2025 Statcast via Baseball Savant), aggregated to one row per pitcher and pitch type (min. 100 pitches).

    Comps. Standardized distance on velocity, induced vertical break, horizontal break, spin, release height and extension within the same pitch family; movement weighted most. Missing measurements (e.g. no release data) are left out of the distance.

    Whiff model. Gradient-boosted classifier trained on 336,333 MLB swings to predict a miss from velocity, spin, movement, release point, extension, arm angle and separation from the pitcher's primary fastball. Validated on pitchers held out of training (ROC AUC 0.641). Every bullpen pitch is scored individually, so date filtering re-averages exact model outputs. Location and count are left out on purpose, so the score reflects the pitch itself ("stuff").
